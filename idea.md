Comprehensive Platform Proposal: Next-Gen Verified C2C & B2C Marketplace1. Executive SummaryThe platform is an enterprise-grade C2C and B2C classifieds application designed to mirror the core functionalities, category depth, and operational mechanics of platforms like Bikroy.com. The core innovation and competitive moat is the Symmetric Dual-Sided NID Verification Engine. By requiring mandatory government-issued National ID (NID) and liveness/biometric verification for both buyers and sellers prior to active trading, the platform completely eliminates anonymous profiles, advance-payment fraud, and fraudulent listings.2. Core Identity & Security Architecture (The Core Difference)[User Registration] ➔ [OTP Phone Check] ➔ [NID / Smart Card Scan + Selfie Match] ➔ [Verified Profile Badge] ➔ [Full Platform Access]
A. Dual-Sided Mandatory NID Verification EngineUniversal Enforcement: Applies strictly to both buyers and sellers. Unverified users can only browse listings in read-only mode.e-KYC & Liveness Integration:OCR extraction of NID Number, Date of Birth, and Full Legal Name from uploaded photos/camera scans.Real-time facial liveness detection (selfie check) to prevent impersonation using lost/stolen NIDs.1-to-1 Identity Binding: One NID is strictly locked to one verified mobile number and bank/mFS payout identity. Duplicate account creation by previously banned users is systemically impossible.Verified Badge & Public Credibility: Displays a dynamic, anti-tamper badge (NID Verified Account) across profile pages, product listings, and chat headers.Legal Accountability Contract: Acceptance of Terms of Service explicitly links fraudulent activity, illegal product listings, or advance scam attempts directly to the user's legal identity for swift law enforcement reporting.3.
